Manager notes for your Nine Cat War Room

What this is
* Vikas's read of eight managers in the league from his calls and chats, as of Nov 11 2026
* Who answers fast, who counters, who wants what, who will not move which player, and how to pitch each one
* Your own team is not in the file. Vikas's team is not in it either

How to load it
1. Copy manager-notes-starter.json out of this folder to somewhere else on your computer
2. Open your site, go to Pickups and trades, then Trades
3. Find Your private manager notes and press Load notes file
4. Pick the file. The trades page reranks by what each manager values

Keep it private
* This starter file is in the public repo because Vikas chose to share it. Anyone who finds the repo can read it
* Once you add your own notes, keep your copy outside the project folder and never push it. Your own reads of the league should stay yours
* The notes stay in your browser only. Load the file again on each device you use

Make it yours
* The file has no deals, no opinions on your players and no fair or win setting. Those were about Vikas's team, so they were left out
* To get the full search of every deal shape with a manager, add "deal":"fair" to that manager in the file and load it again
* Add what a manager thinks of your players under like, dislike and doubt
* Add your own offers under talks. notes-example.json in the repo shows the shape
* These are one person's reads. Check them against what you hear yourself
